## Phase 1 checks

Verify booked reservation check-in/completion, no-show court release, owner staff/coach creation, class roster attendance, and the `/today`, `/finance/balances`, and `/reports/operations` endpoints. Verify coaches see assigned classes only and cannot access staff, customer directory, or finance workflows.

## Phase 2 customer self-service checks

Run against a non-production test venue first. Use a venue with an active
customer account and note its slug, a public court, a future date inside the
booking window, and an active class. Use a disposable customer account; do not
use a real customer's password in smoke testing.

- [ ] Staff login works; staff can open Settings and confirm the intended `STAFF_ONLY`, `REQUEST_APPROVAL`, or `AUTO_CONFIRM` mode.
- [ ] Customer login works at `/portal/:slug/login`; customer home loads.
- [ ] Existing customer authorization is self-scoped: customer can read own profile/activities, while a changed reservation/customer ID returns not found or forbidden.
- [ ] `/book/:slug` public venue page loads with allow-listed venue/court data.
- [ ] Customer portal availability returns only public courts, valid local-date slots, and policy-allowed durations.
- [ ] In `REQUEST_APPROVAL`, customer booking creates a visible `REQUESTED` activity and does not occupy the court until staff confirmation.
- [ ] In `AUTO_CONFIRM`, customer booking creates an immediate reservation and occupies the slot atomically.
- [ ] Customer upcoming activities show the reservation or pending request.
- [ ] Customer cancellation succeeds before cutoff, changes the reservation to `CANCELLED`, and keeps history visible.
- [ ] Schedule/customer availability shows the cancelled slot available again.
- [ ] Class discovery lists active classes, capacity, price, and current enrollment state.
- [ ] Customer can join a full-class waitlist and see it under customer waitlists.
- [ ] Staff Waitlists view lists the same organization-scoped entry and shows current actionable state.
- [ ] Staff can manually fulfill or expire the waitlist; fulfillment uses normal reservation/capacity conflict handling and no automatic customer notification occurs.
- [ ] Activation and reset links can be generated by staff, copied, used once, and rejected after expiry/reuse.

Confirm logs contain request IDs and actor/organization identifiers but no
passwords, bearer tokens, or raw activation/reset tokens. Repeat the booking
and cancellation checks around the venue timezone and cancellation cutoff.
